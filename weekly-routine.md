# Owner-operator weekly routine

About 20 minutes, usually the day the settlement arrives. Copy this list into
a notes app or print it, one copy per week.

Week ending: __________

- [ ] **1. Save the settlement.** Download it as a PDF and put it in this
      week's folder. It is the only document that shows your gross pay and
      every deduction.
- [ ] **2. Book it gross.** Linehaul and fuel surcharge as revenue, each
      deduction as its own expense, escrow as an asset. Booking only the
      deposit hides your real revenue and most of your costs.
- [ ] **3. Match the deposit.** Check that the net on the settlement is the
      amount that hit the bank. This catches a missed load, a double charge or
      a settlement that never arrived.
- [ ] **4. Log fuel outside the settlement.** Receipts for fuel you bought
      yourself, with state and gallons. IFTA needs fuel by state, not just a
      dollar total.
- [ ] **5. Move your pay.** Transfer what you pay yourself, plus a set-aside
      for tax. This keeps owner pay out of expenses and leaves money for
      quarterly estimates.

## Once a month

- [ ] Reconcile the business checking account to its statement.
- [ ] Reconcile the business card and fuel card to their statements.
- [ ] Split the truck payment: interest to expense, principal against the loan.
- [ ] Work out cost per mile (see `cost-per-mile.csv`) and compare it with the
      rates you are taking.

Skipping a week is fine. Skipping a quarter is how people end up paying for
catch-up work.
